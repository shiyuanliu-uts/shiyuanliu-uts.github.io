    Shiyuan Liu is currently a PhD student at University of Technology Sydney (UTS), supervised by Prof. Ying Zhang, Prof. Lu Qin, and Dr. Jianwei Wang. He received his B.Eng. degree in Computer Science and Technology and M.Eng. degree in Information and Communication Engineering from Zhejiang Gongshang University.

    His current research focuses on AI-powered data preparation and management, particularly leveraging LLM-augmented graph learning for the accurate and efficient discovery, integration, and quality management of large-scale tabular data.
